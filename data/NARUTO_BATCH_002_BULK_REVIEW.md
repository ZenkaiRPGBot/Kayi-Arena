## 1. Revised rarity rubric (RARITY-RUBRIC-0.2, provisional)

**Problem with 0.1:** Legendary needed three roles at 85 or more, so only versatile characters could reach it. Apex fighters and extraordinary specialists had no route.

**Rarity Points (RP)** use the SCORE-1.0 role scores, which the engine must recompute:

| Component | Value | Measures |
|---|---|---|
| Peak role score | 0–100 | Peak suitability, blended with Power by the role weights |
| Combat bonus | Power 5: +5, Power 4: +3, else 0 | Canonical strength |
| Specialist bonus | +3 if any Strategist, Support or Healer Fit is 5 | Extraordinary specialization |
| Versatility bonus | +2 per other role scoring 80 or more | Versatility |

| Tier | RP |
|---|---|
| Legendary | 100 or more |
| Epic | 90–99 |
| Rare | 80–89 |
| Uncommon | 60–79 |
| Common | below 60 |

**Scarcity floor (unchanged, provisional):** Healer Fit 4 sets a minimum of Rare and Fit 5 a minimum of Epic.

**Routes to Legendary:**
- *Apex:* Power 5 with a Fit 5 role (RP 105 or more).
- *Extraordinary specialist:* Power 4 with Fit 5 in Strategist, Support or Healer (RP 103).
- *Versatile:* a peak of about 90 plus the combat bonus and three or four other strong roles.

Power 5 alone cannot reach Legendary, and no route requires three elite roles. Ratings are never changed to move a tier, and no quotas apply. A Power 3 or lower specialist tops out at Epic.

## 3. Controversial cases

- **Jinchuriki and Ten-Tails states (Obito, Madara in batch 001).** Excluded as one-time external transformations for consistency. Including Obito's would make him Power 5.
- **Fatal techniques versus Healer (Nagato, Chiyo).** Rinne Tensei and Selfless Reincarnation revive others but are fatal, so they are excluded. Counting them would give Healer 5 and Legendary or Epic by the scarcity floor. Needs an owner ruling.
- **Edo-only and flashback-only characters (Mu, Gengetsu, Third Raikage, Third Kazekage, Tobirama, Hanzo, Shisui, Kushina).** Living primes are inferred from Edo Tensei or flashbacks, so confidence is low.
- **Held records (Hagoromo, Hamura, Indra, Ashura, Sakumo, Izuna).** Evidence is too thin or eligibility is unclear (spirit state). Ratings are placeholders.
- **Tailed beasts and summons.** Independent beasts are rated from sparse independent evidence. Partner and summoner events are shared, and Gamabunta and Manda mostly act under a summoner.
- **Gaara after extraction.** Shukaku-dependent defense excluded, so Tank is 4, not 5.
- **Might Guy.** Gates through the Seventh only; Attacker 4 and Power 4 may be 5 if the Gates are verified.
- **Minato.** RP lands just under Epic; a single rating change would move him.
- **Hashirama, Kaguya, Hagoromo.** Power 5 and Fit 5 ratings need independent review, and Hagoromo's evidence is spirit-state only.
